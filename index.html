Welcome to Mill Creek Inn at River Run

Nestled along the tranquil waters of Snake River in the heart of Keystone, Colorado, Mill Creek Inn offers an unparalleled blend of mountain serene beauty and world-class resort convenience. Located just steps from the lively atmosphere of River Run Village, our condominiums provide the ideal mountain retreat for winter ski enthusiasts and summer adventure seekers alike.

Whether you are returning from a day on the slopes, exploring vibrant autumn gold aspen groves, or relaxing by the stream, Mill Creek Inn surrounds you with classic Rocky Mountain hospitality and unforgettable views in every season.

Prime Location

Walkable access to the River Run Gondola, dining, and village events.

Streamfront Views

Peaceful views overlooking wetlands, mountains, and Mill Creek.

Year-Round Beauty

Experience pristine winter powder, crisp fall foliage, and vibrant summers.
